How it works

Drag an item from the toolbox onto the Mola Mola to feed it.

  • Seaweed: log a period

    Feed the Mola Mola seaweed to record a period. Choose the day it started, then the day it ended, or pick "Still going" if it hasn't finished. Feed it seaweed again later to add the end date.

  • Jellyfish: predict your next period

    Feed the Mola Mola a jellyfish and it will tell you how many days until your next period is expected, or how many days overdue it is.

  • Fish: view your history

    Feed the Mola Mola a fish to open your cycle history. See every period you've logged along with your average period and cycle length, and edit or delete any entry.
