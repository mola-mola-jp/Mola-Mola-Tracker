Mola Mola Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Summary

Mola Mola Tracker does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

The dates of the periods you log are saved in the app's local storage on your device so they are there the next time you open it. They are never sent to us or to any third party, and there are no accounts, sign-ins, advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind.

Your control

You can edit or delete any entry from the app's history screen. Deleting the app (or clearing its website data in your browser) permanently removes all stored data from your device. Because nothing is stored on a server, we cannot recover deleted data.

Not medical advice

Predictions shown in the app are simple estimates based on the dates you have logged. They are not medical advice and should not be used for contraception or to diagnose or treat any condition. Please speak to a healthcare professional about any health concerns.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
